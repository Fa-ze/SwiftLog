About SwiftLog

SwiftLog was built with one goal in mind to make the SIWES experience simpler, faster and more organized for Nigerian University students.

Every year, hundreds of 200 and 300 level students undergoing their SIWES are stuck carrying physical logbooks that get damaged, lost or delayed. Supervisors wait weeks to review entries. Students scramble to rewrite lost work.

SwiftLog eliminates all of that. Students log daily activities digitally from any device. Supervisors review and approve entries instantly online. No paper. No damage. No delays.

Built by a SIWES student, for SIWES students. 💪
